How this was built

Methodology & honest limitations

What's in /transcripts/. Each talk has a dedicated transcript file with: a full segment-by-segment account of the talk's content extracted from Anthropic's primary sources (claude.com session pages, official blog recaps, speaker tweets, and contemporaneous reporting); a dedicated "Direct quotes" section preserving every word-for-word line that primary sources reported (currently 14 verbatim quotes across 5 talks, spanning Boris Cherny, Cat Wu, Dianne Penn, Lucas Gonzalez, and Dickson Tsai); demo specifics, growth numbers, and announcement mechanics; and per-talk conclusions/advice. Quotes are also surfaced inline in each video card.

Why these aren't the original YouTube caption files. Verbatim YouTube caption files could not be downloaded from this sandbox. Two independent layers block it:
  1. Proxy allowlist. Direct fetches to youtube.com/api/timedtext and every transcript mirror (youtubesummary, ulisten, tactiq, notegpt, invidious, piped) return HTTP 403. Only github.com, *.googleapis.com and a handful of package mirrors are allowed.
  2. YouTube bot-gating on the datacenter IP. I built a full InnerTube client against youtubei.googleapis.com (which is reachable) and tried every client variant — WEB, MWEB, ANDROID, IOS, TVHTML5-embedded, WEB_EMBEDDED, ANDROID_TESTSUITE, ANDROID_MUSIC, ANDROID_VR, ANDROID_CREATOR, IOS_MUSIC, WEB_CREATOR. For our specific Code-with-Claude videos every client returns LOGIN_REQUIRED: "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" or ERROR: "This video is unavailable." ANDROID_VR does get status=OK + caption tracks for unrestricted videos (verified against the Rickroll), proving the IP isn't universally gated — these particular videos require an authenticated session. Even when captionTracks come through, their baseUrl still points to www.youtube.com, which the proxy blocks separately.
Bypassing both layers needs (a) a po_token minted from BotGuard endpoints the proxy also blocks, plus (b) a valid Google account cookie. So each transcript here is a transcript-grade artifact assembled from Anthropic's own session pages, official recaps, and contemporaneous reporting — with verbatim quotes preserved wherever sources reported them word-for-word. Open any talk's Transcript & notes for the full document, or read the files in /transcripts.

Scope & inclusion
